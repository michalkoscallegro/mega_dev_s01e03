0002 · Duplicate reports share a verified cause

owner michalkoscallegro accepted reviewed 2026-11-12 · 0 days ago

Context

The desk serves multiple products. Normalized subjects identify similar wording, not a shared failure. The #4471 follow-up shows that the old rule merged two defects into one known ticket and hid the second fix.

Decision

This decision supersedes decision 0001; that accepted record remains unchanged.

Read the report and identify the affected product, tenant’s authoritative configuration, entry point, reproduction, and failure mechanism. Merge only when the evidence supports the same cause within the relevant product. Record the evidence and retain each original report ID. Subjects and browser-derived intake tags are search hints, not merge keys.

For sched-prod date reports in this export, use the existing causeOf and sameCause functions with tenantOf: day-first booking-v3 form reports are hint, while other day-first entry points are parser. These rules describe this incident, not every product or every future date defect.

Missing tenant information, unsupported failure paths, and contradictory accounts need investigation. Keep them separate until there is evidence; never infer equality from two unknown causes. A new verified cause gets its own issue.

The examples cover identical subjects with different causes and different subjects with the same cause. Unknown tenants do not match even when their subjects are identical. The current triage page is the desk’s entry point for this rule.

Consequences

Triage requires reading and checking configuration. It avoids hiding a second defect behind a known ticket. Count reports once by ID, preserve contrary evidence, and separate receipt time from merge time when reviewing an incident.
