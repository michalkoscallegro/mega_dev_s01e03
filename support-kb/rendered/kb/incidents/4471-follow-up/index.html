Shared parser: month first instead of the tenant’s date convention

The parser issue covers day-first tenants using app or email, and booking-v2 forms. For example, R-41200 from harbour.dental describes 7 April becoming 4 July. The tenant is en-GB even if a reporter’s browser is set otherwise. R-41205 from morrow.tax is an app report with tenant fr-FR, despite browser en-US and intake tag hint. It belongs to parser.

Interpret the original input using the tenant convention, and verify the resulting date. Preserve month-first behavior. Do not mark the independent form-hint issue fixed when this parser is corrected.

booking-v3: the displayed hint disagrees with form parsing

The hint issue covers form reports from alder.physio (de-DE), copper.studio (es-ES), and pale.harbour (pt-PT). They run booking-v3, which displays MM/DD/YYYY while reading input day first. R-41230 describes 6 January becoming 1 June; intake incorrectly tagged it parser. Following that hint with 01/06/2026 reproduces the mismatch.

The original corrective option was to generate the hint from the same tenant convention used to read the input. Engineering instead retired booking-v3 and chose won’t fix. App and email reports from these tenants are not automatically hint reports: channel matters.

When it happened

Reports arrived before the desk merged them, and continued after it did. A large known ticket and a queued fix are not evidence of resolution. Compare receivedAt with mergedAt before interpreting a spike.

Change the running desk rule, not just this incident

Decision 0002 replaces subject-only merging. Current triage now asks for product, tenant configuration, entry point, and evidence of a shared cause. Equal subjects can hide different defects; different subjects can describe the same defect. Unknowns stay separate for investigation.

Decision 0001 remains unchanged as the historical accepted decision. This incident is an example of why it is superseded, not permission to apply a scheduling-specific classifier blindly to every product the support desk handles.
